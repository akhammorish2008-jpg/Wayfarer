# ✈️ Wayfarer — Your Travel Planning Companion

**Wayfarer** is a simple, user-friendly travel planning website designed to make organising trips easier. It brings trip planning, itinerary organisation and budget estimation together in one place.

## 🌍 Live Demo

[Visit Wayfarer](https://akhammorish2008-jpg.github.io/Wayfarer/)

## ✨ Features

- **Trip Planning:** Enter your destination, travel dates and trip duration.
- **Travel Preferences:** Choose from different travel styles, including balanced, nature, culture and relaxed.
- **Itinerary:** Organise your trip into morning, afternoon and evening activities.
- **Useful Links:** Access maps, hotel information and restaurant options.
- **Budget Calculator:** Estimate accommodation, food, transportation and activity expenses.
- **Expense Breakdown:** View total, per-person and per-day costs.
- **Multiple Currencies:** Supports USD, EUR, GBP and INR.
- **Copy Itinerary:** Easily copy your planned itinerary.
- **Local Storage:** Saves your trip details in the browser.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5** — Website structure.
- **CSS3** — Styling, layout, animations and responsive design.
- **JavaScript** — Interactive features, trip planning and budget calculations.
- **Local Storage** — Saving user preferences in the browser.

## 🚀 Getting Started

You can access the website directly through the live demo.

To view the source code locally:

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your web browser.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of Wayfarer is to provide a convenient and accessible platform where users can organise their travel plans and estimate expenses without needing multiple planning tools.
